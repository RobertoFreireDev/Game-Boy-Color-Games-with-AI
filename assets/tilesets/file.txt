assets/tilesets/ — ts_*.c: BG tiles (max 128 per scene) + the BG palettes they use (max 7, slots 0-6).
Made from source_art/mapsheets/*.png when the human supplies an image. Comment every tile with its
index and name: maps refer to tiles by index. count = number of tiles written.
Full conventions: CLAUDE.md sections 6.4 and 14.
