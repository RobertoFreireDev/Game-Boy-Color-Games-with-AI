source_art/mapsheets/ — PNG maps and tile sheets pasted by the human. The AI cuts each map into 8x8
tiles, removes duplicates (flipped copies included), and writes assets/tilesets/ts_<tileset>.c plus
assets/maps/map_<name>.c (ASCII rows + legend). PNGs here are source files only: the build never
reads them. Full process: CLAUDE.md section 14.

---- what to put here ---------------------------------------------------------
<name>.png   a whole map (level, title screen, menu background) or a tile sheet
<name>.txt   notes for the AI (optional, but game meaning such as solid/hazard/spawn can't be seen)

---- how to draw the PNG ------------------------------------------------------
- 1 image pixel = 1 Game Boy pixel. Upscaled art is fine if the notes say "scale: N" (whole numbers).
- Width and height are multiples of 8. Whole maps: 160x144 (one screen, 20x18 tiles) up to
  1600x1600 (200x200 tiles). Tile art must line up with the 8x8 grid starting at the top-left corner.
- Each 8x8 cell uses at most 4 colors, all from one palette.
- The whole map uses at most 7 palettes of 4 colors and at most 128 different 8x8 tiles
  (a tile and its mirror image count once). Repeat tiles: that's what keeps the count low.
- Leave characters, enemies and items out of the map: they are sprites. Mark where they start in the notes.
- A tile sheet (tiles in a grid, no map) is also fine; then the AI draws the map in ASCII from the notes.

---- forest1.txt (example notes) ----------------------------------------------
type: map               (map | tiles | title)
tileset: forest         (maps that share a tileset name share one ts_forest.c; max 128 tiles together)
scale: 1
solid: brown bricks, grey stone blocks
oneway: wooden planks
hazard: red spikes, water
over: the fence bars (drawn in front of sprites)
spawn: player at tile (3, 20); enemies at (18, 20), (40, 12); coins at (10, 15), (11, 15)
trigger: door at tile (76, 18) = level exit
